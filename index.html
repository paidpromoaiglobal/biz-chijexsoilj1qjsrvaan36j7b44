About Us

Dr.Swathi's Family Dental Clinic

Dr. Swathi's Family Dental Clinic is a trusted dental practice located at Panneer Nursing Home, Thiruthanthoni Road, Woraiyur, Tiruchirappalli. Led by the highly skilled and patient-centric Dr. Swathi, the clinic offers comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages in a warm, welcoming environment. With a perfect 5-star rating from satisfied patients, the clinic is known for its gentle approach, transparent treatment planning, and pocket-friendly services.

At Dr. Swathi's Family Dental Clinic, patient education and comfort are at the core of every visit. Dr. Swathi takes the time to explain procedures using audio-visual aids and provides free oral health education materials, ensuring every patient feels informed and at ease. From routine check-ups to advanced procedures like root canal treatments and crown replacements, the clinic delivers exceptional dental care backed by diligent follow-up and genuine concern for long-term oral health.
